# BUMI / BLOOM
## Brand & Marketplace Design Guide

> A modern Indonesian fashion marketplace for everyday men’s and women’s clothing.
> Simple, warm, approachable, and visually engaging — with **Cream** as the main brand tone.

---

## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** BUMI / BLOOM  
**Category:** Fashion marketplace  
**Origin:** Indonesia  
**Primary Audience:** Men and women aged 18–40  
**Positioning:** Curated Indonesian fashion that feels modern, wearable, and accessible.

### Brand Idea

**BUMI** represents local roots, natural simplicity, and Indonesian identity.  
**BLOOM** represents self-expression, confidence, and personal growth through style.

The marketplace should feel like a calm editorial fashion space rather than a crowded discount platform.

### Suggested Brand Lockup

**BUMI / BLOOM**  
*Rooted here. Made to bloom.*

Alternative tagline options:

- **Wear Your Everyday**
- **Made Here, Worn Everywhere**
- **Style That Feels Like You**
- **Local Roots, Modern Style**

## 3. Colour Palette

The visual direction is based on the supplied colour palette.

### Primary Colour

#### Cream — Main Brand Tone

- **Hex:** `#F2EEE9`
- **Usage:** Main website background, packaging, cards, editorial sections, product detail pages.
- **Feeling:** Warm, clean, calm, natural.

Cream should cover approximately **60–70%** of the visual experience.

### Core Supporting Colours

#### Dark Blue

- **Hex:** `#00408C`
- **Usage:** Main typography, navigation, primary buttons, logo, footer.
- **Feeling:** Trustworthy, bold, modern.

#### Red

- **Hex:** `#E85234`
- **Usage:** Promotional labels, sale badges, active states, campaign highlights.
- **Feeling:** Energetic, expressive, youthful.

### Soft Accent Colours

#### Blue

- **Hex:** `#96ADD6`
- **Usage:** Secondary sections, men’s collection campaigns, filters, information blocks.

#### Peach

- **Hex:** `#F9B8AF`
- **Usage:** Women’s collection campaigns, seasonal banners, editorial accents.

#### Pink

- **Hex:** `#F2D7D3`
- **Usage:** Soft section backgrounds, hover states, loyalty or gifting pages.

### Recommended Colour Ratio

| Colour | Approximate Usage |
|---|---:|
| Cream | 65% |
| Dark Blue | 20% |
| Red | 7% |
| Blue | 3% |
| Peach | 3% |
| Pink | 2% |

Use for:

- Product details

- Large editorial headlines
- Short sentences
- Comfortable line spacing
- Avoid excessive uppercase text

## 10. Backend, CMS & Admin Dashboard

The marketplace must include a secure backend so the internal team can manage products, brands, categories, images, links, editorial content, and marketplace operations without editing code.

### Admin Roles and Permissions

Recommended access levels:

| Role | Main Permissions |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Full access, user roles, settings, deletion, audit logs |
| Marketplace Admin | Products, brands, categories, sellers, orders, promotions |
| Content Editor | Homepage, Journal, banners, images, links, SEO content |
| Seller / Brand Admin | Manage only their own brand, products, inventory, and orders |
| Customer Support | View orders and customers; update support-related order status |

Use role-based access control so sensitive actions such as deleting a brand, changing payment settings, or publishing site-wide content are limited to authorised users.

### Dashboard Overview

The admin homepage should display:

- Total sales and orders
- Products live, draft, and out of stock
- Active and pending brands
- Low-stock alerts
- Pending seller approvals
- Recent content changes
- Top-performing categories and brands
- Quick actions: **Add Product**, **Add Brand**, **Add Category**, and **Upload Image**

### Content Editing

Provide a simple visual editor for homepage sections, campaign pages, brand stories, Journal articles, FAQs, and policy pages.

The editor should support:

- Edit headings, body copy, buttons, labels, and captions
- Rich text and Markdown editing
- Draft, preview, publish, unpublish, and scheduled publishing
- Reorder page sections with drag and drop
- Add internal links and external backlinks
- Open links in the same tab or a new tab
- Set link title and accessible label
- Apply `nofollow`, `sponsored`, or `ugc` attributes where relevant
- Edit page slug, meta title, meta description, and social sharing image
- Revision history and restore previous versions

### Image and Media Library

Allow authorised users to upload and manage images from the backend.

Required functions:

- Upload JPG, PNG, WebP, SVG, and short-form video where supported
- Multi-image upload and drag-and-drop upload
- Automatic image compression and responsive image sizes
- Crop, resize, set focal point, replace, archive, and delete images
- Add alt text, caption, copyright/credit, and searchable tags
- Select an existing image from the media library
- Reorder product gallery images
- Assign separate desktop and mobile campaign images
- Warn before deleting an image currently used by a product or page

Recommended product image ratio: `4:5`. Campaign banners may use separate desktop and mobile ratios.

### Brand Management

Admins must be able to add, edit, publish, archive, and delete brands.

Each brand record should include:

- Brand name
- Unique slug
- Logo and cover image
- Short and full description
- Country and city of origin
- Men, women, or unisex audience
- Brand categories
- Official website backlink
- Instagram, TikTok, and other social links
- Seller or account owner
- Verification status
- Active, draft, pending, suspended, or archived status
- SEO title, description, and social image
- Featured-brand toggle and display order

#### Brand Deletion Rules

- Use soft deletion or archive by default so records can be restored.
- Show the number of linked products before deletion.
- Do not permanently delete a brand while active products, open orders, or financial records depend on it.
- Provide options to archive its products or move them to another brand before final deletion.
- Permanent deletion should be restricted to Super Admin.

### Category Management

Admins must be able to create, edit, reorder, merge, archive, and delete categories.

Support a hierarchical category structure, for example:

```text
Women
├── Clothing
│   ├── Dresses
│   ├── Shirts & Blouses
│   ├── Trousers
│   └── Outerwear
└── Accessories

Men
├── Clothing
│   ├── Shirts
│   ├── T-Shirts
│   ├── Trousers
│   └── Outerwear
└── Accessories
```

Each category should contain:

- Category name
- Parent category
- Unique slug
- Description
- Thumbnail or banner image
- Menu visibility
- Homepage visibility
- Display order
- Active or archived status
- SEO title, description, and social image
- Default filters and applicable product attributes

#### Category Deletion Rules

- Display the number of assigned products and subcategories before deletion.
- Require products to be reassigned, uncategorised, or archived first.
- Allow category merging to preserve product organisation and SEO value.
- Support URL redirects from an old category slug to a replacement category.

### Product Management

Admins and authorised sellers should be able to:

- Add, edit, duplicate, publish, unpublish, archive, and delete products
- Assign a brand and one or more categories
- Upload and reorder product images
- Add product description, materials, care instructions, and size guide
- Create colour and size variants
- Set SKU, barcode, price, compare-at price, cost, stock, and low-stock threshold
- Add product tags and collection assignments
- Add internal product links and external reference or brand backlinks
- Set New, Bestseller, Low Stock, Made in Indonesia, and Sale badges
- Preview the product page before publishing
- Schedule product launches
- Bulk edit prices, stock, categories, tags, and publication status
- Import and export product data by CSV

Products should use draft, pending review, published, rejected, archived, and out-of-stock states.

### Homepage and Navigation Builder

The backend should allow the content team to manage:

- Announcement bar
- Main navigation and dropdown menus
- Homepage hero
- Category tiles
- New-arrival sections
- BUMI Edit features
- Brand spotlight
- Shop-the-mood sections
- Promotional banners
- Newsletter block
- Footer links and social links

Each module should support visibility dates, audience targeting where applicable, image selection, button label, destination link, and display order.

### Link and Backlink Management

Provide a consistent link field throughout the CMS for buttons, images, articles, brand profiles, products, menus, and banners.

Link settings should include:

- Internal page selector or external URL
- Link text
- Accessible label
- Same-tab or new-tab behaviour
- `nofollow`, `sponsored`, and `ugc` options
- Link validation and broken-link warnings
- UTM campaign parameters
- Redirect management when slugs change

External links opened in a new tab should automatically use secure `noopener noreferrer` behaviour.

### Seller Management

Marketplace admins should be able to:

- Review and approve seller applications
- Create or connect seller accounts to brands
- Request missing legal or banking documents
- Suspend or reactivate seller access
- Set seller commission and payout rules
- Review submitted products before publication
- View seller performance, returns, cancellations, and customer ratings

A seller should only be able to edit content belonging to their approved brand or account.

### Audit, Safety, and Recovery

The backend should include:

- Audit log showing who created, edited, published, archived, or deleted records
- Confirmation dialogs for destructive actions
- Soft deletion and restore where practical
- Automatic backups
- Login protection and optional two-factor authentication
- Session expiry and device management
- Validation for duplicate slugs, incomplete products, invalid links, and missing image alt text
- Activity notifications for important changes

### Suggested Core Data Entities

```text
User
Role
Seller
Brand
Category
Product
ProductVariant
ProductImage
Collection
Page
ContentBlock
MediaAsset
Link
Order
OrderItem
Customer
Promotion
Redirect
AuditLog
```

Key relationships:

- A seller may manage one or more brands.
- A brand has many products.
- A product belongs to one brand and may belong to multiple categories.
- A category may have a parent category and multiple child categories.
- A product has multiple variants and images.
- Pages contain reorderable content blocks, media assets, and links.

### Minimum Backend Acceptance Criteria

The backend is considered ready for initial launch when an authorised admin can:

1. Log in securely and access only permitted modules.
2. Add, edit, preview, publish, archive, restore, and delete content.
3. Upload images, edit metadata, and assign images to products, brands, categories, and pages.
4. Insert internal links and external backlinks with appropriate link attributes.
5. Add, edit, reorder, archive, restore, and safely delete brands.
6. Create parent and child categories, assign products, reorder categories, and safely delete or merge categories.
7. Add and edit products, variants, prices, stock, images, brand, and categories.
8. View an audit trail for important administrative actions.
9. Manage the main homepage sections and navigation without developer assistance.

## 11. UI Design Language

### Layout

- Wide margins
- Generous white space
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Editorial image blocks
- Maximum content width: `1280–1440px`
- 12-column desktop grid
- 4-column mobile grid

### Buttons

#### Primary Button

- Background: `#00408C`
- Text: `#F2EEE9`
- Border radius: `999px` or `8px`
- Padding: `14px 24px`

#### Accent Button

- Background: `#E85234`
- Text: `#F2EEE9`

#### Secondary Button

- Background: transparent
- Border: `1px solid #00408C`
- Text: `#00408C`

### Corners and Shadows

- Cards: `8–12px` radius
- Buttons: either soft rectangular or pill-shaped
- Shadows should be very subtle
- Avoid glassmorphism and excessive gradients

## 17. Suggested Homepage Design Prompt

> Create a minimalist and engaging fashion marketplace named **BUMI / BLOOM**, based in Indonesia and focused mainly on men’s and women’s clothing. Use warm Cream `#F2EEE9` as the dominant background colour, Dark Blue `#00408C` for typography and primary buttons, and Red `#E85234` as the main accent. Use Peach, Pink, and Soft Blue sparingly for campaign sections. The design should combine large editorial serif headlines with clean sans-serif body text, spacious layouts, high-quality fashion photography, soft rounded corners, and minimal shadows. The overall feeling should be warm, modern, curated, inclusive, and locally rooted. Avoid clutter, aggressive discount styling, or overly luxurious visuals.

---

## 18. Brand Summary

**BUMI / BLOOM** is a calm, contemporary marketplace for Indonesian fashion. Its Cream-led visual identity creates warmth and approachability, while Dark Blue provides trust and structure. Red adds energy without overwhelming the design. The experience should feel curated, easy to navigate, and relevant to modern men and women looking for thoughtful everyday clothing.
