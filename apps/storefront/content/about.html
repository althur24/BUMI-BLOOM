Our story

BUMI / BLOOM started with a simple idea: the joyful, well-made kids’ clothing coming out of small Indonesian studios deserves a place on Australian shelves — and in family wardrobes from Brisbane to Perth.

We’re a small, family-run business, not a faceless conglomerate. Every order supports real people: the independent labels we partner with, the studios that cut and sew each piece, and the families who wear them.

We keep the edit short and considered. Playful, comfortable pieces in soft fabrics that are made to last and made to play in — plus screen-free busy books for quiet-time play.

Sustainability, honestly

  • Real fabrics, clearly listed. Every product page names the material so you know exactly what you’re buying.
  • Non-toxic, azo-free dyes on the pieces where this is stated.
  • Made to last and made to play in. We favour pieces that survive hand-me-downs rather than fast-fashion throwaways.
  • Smaller, considered collections. Our partner studios work in smaller runs, which means less waste and more care per piece.

Why local matters

Choosing local kids’ brands creates a ripple effect across communities — from the pattern maker to the seamstress to the small business owner shipping your parcel.

It also means shorter distances, closer relationships and the ability to ask questions about how something was made. If you ever want to know more about a piece or a label, email us and we’ll find out for you.
