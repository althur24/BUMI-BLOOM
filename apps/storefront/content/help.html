Size guide

Every product page has its own Size Guide tab listing the sizes we stock for that piece. Many styles also show an age hint in the size options (for example “M (3–4 YO)”).

  • Measure your child’s chest and height, then match it to the closest size.
  • Children grow at different speeds, so age is only a guide — chest width matters most.
  • Between two sizes? We recommend sizing up for room to grow.

Still unsure? Email us the product name plus your child’s measurements and we’ll help you choose.

Shipping & returns

  • Any 3 clothing or accessory items for $69 flat — mix & match, applied automatically in your bag. Busy books are excluded, and items beyond three are charged at their normal price.
  • Free AU shipping on orders over $99, measured after the bundle discount.
  • Standard delivery 5–8 business days across Australia.
  • 30-day returns on unworn pieces in their original condition.
  • Faulty or damaged on arrival? Tell us within 14 days for a refund or replacement.

The 3-for-$69 price cannot be combined with a discount code. Shipping is calculated at checkout and confirmed before you pay. All prices are in AUD.

FAQs

We deliver Australia-wide. Standard delivery takes 5–8 business days, and shipping is free on orders over $99.

Your confirmation email includes a tracking link. You can also open it any time — it shows your order status, items and delivery progress.

Email us as soon as you can. If your order hasn’t been packed yet we’ll do our best to change or cancel it.

Unworn pieces can be returned within 30 days. If something arrives faulty or damaged, tell us within 14 days and we’ll refund or replace it.

Add any three clothing or accessory items to your bag and they are repriced to a flat $69 automatically — no code needed. Mix sizes, colours and brands freely. Busy books are not part of the offer: they do not count towards the three items and are charged at their normal price. Any fourth eligible item onwards also keeps its normal price, and the offer cannot be combined with a discount code.

Contact us

Questions about an order, a size or a return? Email hello@bumibloom.com and we’ll get back to you. We aim to reply within one business day.

For anything about a placed order, include your order number so we can help faster.

Privacy

This is a plain-language summary of how we handle your information.

  • Newsletter: if you join our list we store your email address so we can send occasional updates. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to be removed at any time.
  • Orders: shipping details and order information are shared with our store and payment partners (Shopify and Stripe) only to process and deliver your order. Card details are handled by Stripe — we never see your full card number.
  • Your choices: email us any time to ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted.

Terms

Plain-language summary — for anything you need in writing, email us.

  • All prices are in Australian dollars (AUD) and include any applicable taxes shown at checkout.
  • Orders are subject to stock availability; if an item sells out we’ll contact you and refund it in full.
  • Colours can look slightly different from screen to screen, and handmade pieces may vary a little — that’s part of their charm.
  • Returns are handled as described in Shipping & returns, in addition to your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
